Natter is team chat that feels instant: spaces with text and voice channels, threads, direct messages and calls, on a server you control.

Messages appear the moment you press send, and never post twice, even on a shaky connection. Calls run through Natter's own media server, so they stay smooth with more than a handful of people.

CHAT
• Spaces with text, voice and announcement channels in collapsible categories
• Direct messages and group messages, with calls built in
• Threads for side conversations, started from any message
• Replies, reactions, edits and pins
• @mentions, #channel links, Markdown, spoilers and code blocks
• Unread markers and mention counts that stay in sync across your devices
• Full-text search, files and pictures, link previews and a GIF picker

VOICE AND VIDEO
• Drop into a voice channel, or call anyone from a direct message
• Camera, screen sharing, mute and deafen, speaking indicators
• Choose the quality you receive for each camera and screen share
• Noise suppression, echo cancellation and automatic gain

YOUR SERVER, YOUR DATA
• Natter is free, open-source software (AGPL-3.0). The server is one program and one data folder.
• Works with Nextcloud: sign in with your Nextcloud account, and your files stay in your own cloud.
• No Nextcloud? Natter has its own accounts: your team sends you an invite link.

AND MORE
• Daily Word: a five-letter word game in English or Turkish, with team standings
• Light, dark and colour themes, accent colours, text size and compact mode
• Roles and permissions, private channels, invites that expire

No ads. No tracking. Your messages stay on the server you choose.

To use Natter you need the address of a Natter server, for example from your team. Want to run your own? Everything you need is at github.com/Circuit-Biscuit/natter
